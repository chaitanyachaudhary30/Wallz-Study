## 1. Introduction and game rules

Modified Wallz is a two-player, Quoridor-like pawn race on an N x N grid. Player 0 starts at
`(0, N//2)` and must reach row N-1; player 1 starts at `(N-1, N//2)` and must reach row 0.
On each turn a player makes one action. `wallz_engine.py` is the only source of the rules
(`docs/game_rules.md`, frozen in `docs/reports/stage1_engine_freeze.md`).

| Action | Rule |
|---|---|
| MOVE | one orthogonal step; not across a wall, onto the opponent, onto a blocked cell, or off the board; no pawn jump |
| WALL | a 2-cell wall; costs 1 of the `M` walls; becomes the player's latest wall |
| REPLACE (A) | moves only the player's latest wall; costs nothing; allowed with 0 walls left |
| HOP (B) | once per game; one step across a wall **owned by the opponent** |
| BLOCK (C) | once per game; a cell is impassable for exactly K plies |

Every action must leave a goal path for both pawns (pawns ignored; all blocks count, including
one that expires this ply: team Decision 9). A position whose mover has no legal action is a
draw; a 5-fold repetition is a draw.

**HOP change.** Until commit `4e346a8` (2026-11-08) HOP was a 2-cell jump that needed no wall.
Agents used it as a free sprint, so the team switched to the proposal's rule above. Results made
under the old rule are stale (`STALE.md`).

**Truncation is not a draw.** The match runner stops a game at 200 plies and records
`truncated`, its own outcome. Every table here shows draw rate and truncation rate in separate
columns.

## 3. Agents

### 3.1 Greedy (Member 2)

One-ply lookahead on `H = d_opp - d_self` (breadth-first distances), ties broken by action
category, own progress, then action index (`agents/greedy.py`). Deterministic unless seeded.

### 3.2 Minimax (Member 1)

Negamax with fail-hard alpha-beta, a distance evaluation `U = tanh(0.2 (d_opp - d_self))`,
move ordering and optional iterative deepening under a time limit (`agents/minimax.py`).
Pruning keeps root values exact: maximum difference 0.0 over 118 positions, and it cuts nodes
from 65,697 to 3,576 on 20 N5 positions at depth 2 (`final_minimax/alphabeta_equivalence.json`).
Move ordering saves 8.4% of nodes at depth 2 (95% bootstrap 3.5-14.2%) on 50 N5 positions
(`final_minimax/ordering.json`).

**Hop-aware scorer — new contribution, Wallz-Study [new].** `HopAwareEvaluator`
(`minimax:...,eval=hop`) measures a player's distance as the shortest path crossing at most one
opponent-owned wall while that player's hop is unused. It equals an engine-only search on 240
random positions (`tests/test_minimax_hop_eval.py`), and the default scorer is unchanged: a full
rerun reproduced all 100 main games (`final_minimax_hop/default_repro_diff.json`).

### 3.3 DQN (Member 4)

Negamax Double DQN with a convolution stack (14 input planes, 64 channels, 256 hidden units),
masked arg-max, sparse +1 reward to the winner, gamma 0.99, self-play against the online
network, snapshots and Greedy (`docs/report_sections/member4_dqn.md`). The action mask equals
the engine's validator on all 5,952,580 ids checked (`final_dqn/compat_mask_audit.json`).
**[new]** Retrained under the final rules: 3x3 (K1 M1) seeds 42-46 at 200,000 steps, 4x4 (K1 M0)
seed 42 at 500,000 steps, 5x5 (K3 M10) seed 42 at 500,040 steps (`checkpoints/final/*/manifest.json`,
`final_dqn/5x5/final_dqn_5x5_s42_500k_eval.json`).

## 4. Exact solver and evaluation method

**Solver.** `evaluation/state_graph.py` builds the reachable position graph only through the
engine; `evaluation/retrograde.py` labels each node WIN, LOSS or DRAW for the side to move with
a rank (plies to the goal under best play) by backward attractor propagation, which handles
cycles. A capped graph reports UNKNOWN, never DRAW (`docs/report_sections/member3_eval.md`, 1-2).

**Validation [new].** `tests/test_solver_final_rules.py`, 18 tests, all pass
(`final_eval/test_solver_final_rules.txt`); each edge is exactly one engine `apply_action`; an
independent DFS + value-iteration reference agrees on every position; two broken engines make
4 and 3 tests fail (`final_eval/mutation_check_solver_tests.txt`); the legacy solver agrees on
all 16 N3 cells with 0 mismatches (`final_eval/crosscheck_N3_K1_M0.json`, `crosscheck_N3_K1_M1.json`).

**Match method [new].** Greedy, Minimax without a time limit and DQN are deterministic, so a game
is fixed by its opening. Openings are random pawn moves; the opening length is the shortest with
enough distinct openings, and only the first seed of each distinct opening is played, in both
seatings. Rates and Wilson 95% intervals count distinct games. Factorial effects use a paired
bootstrap over openings with Holm correction over the 7 effects.

## 5. Results

### 5a. Exact small-board results (3x3, 4x4)

These hold only for the solved cells and the position-game model. They say nothing about 5x5 or 9x9.

**Root values** (player 0 to move; `final_eval/solve_*.json`, tables `final_eval/tables/solve_*.md`):

| Setting | Complete | Root | Largest complete cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| N3 K1 M0 | 8 of 8 | WIN in 3 (C off), WIN in 5 (C on) | 642 states |

| N4 K1 M0 | 8 of 8 | LOSS in 6 (C off), LOSS in 8 (C on) | 4,512 states |

exact value, in N3 A1B1C1 K1 M1: Greedy 0.897, Minimax d2 0.953, Minimax d2 hop scorer 0.967,
DQN seeds 42-46 0.953-0.977. Over all non-terminal 3x3 states the five DQN seeds keep the value

`final_dqn/final_dqn_4x4_s42_eval.json`). Against the exact solver all five 3x3 models get the
game value in all 16 distinct openings and seats (4 wins, 8 draws, 4 losses); the 4x4 model loses

## 6. Factorial ablation

N5 K3 M10, `minimax:depth=2` vs `greedy`, all 8 A/B/C cells, both seatings, 200-ply cap. The
**primary** run, fixed beforehand as the shortest opening length with at least 30 distinct
openings, uses 4-ply openings and 40 distinct openings: 640 matches, 640/640 replays verified
(`final_eval/ablation_N5_K3_M10_open4/`). Effects on Minimax's win rate (paired bootstrap over
openings, Holm over 7 tests; table `final_eval/tables/ablation_primary_effects.md`):

| Effect | Estimate | 95% CI | Survives Holm |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A | -0.081 | (-0.147, -0.019) | no |
| **B** | **-0.175** | (-0.259, -0.088) | **yes** |
| C | -0.037 | (-0.072, -0.006) | no |
| AB | -0.037 | (-0.078, +0.003) | no |
| AC | +0.037 | (+0.000, +0.075) | no |
| BC | -0.006 | (-0.047, +0.034) | no |
| ABC | -0.006 | (-0.031, +0.019) | no |

Only B (the hop) has a supported effect on the win rate. It also lowers the draw rate by 0.122
and shortens games by 15.4 plies (`final_eval/ablation_N5_K3_M10_open4/summary.json`
`effects.rule_draw`, `effects.length_plies`). The pilot's AB interaction (+0.11, pre-freeze) is
not supported: -0.037 here.

**Sensitivity checks** (not primary): with 2-ply openings (9 distinct, the pilot's design) B is
-0.403; with 6-ply openings (40 distinct) B is -0.413 and C (-0.088) and BC (+0.056) also survive
Holm (`final_eval/ablation_N5_K3_M10_open2_pilot_comparison/`, `final_eval/ablation_N5_K3_M10_open6/`).
B lowers Minimax's win rate in all three, but its size depends on the openings played.

Power: with 40 openings the smallest win-rate effect detectable with 80% power is 0.157 at
alpha 0.05 and 0.197 at Holm's first step, worst case (`final_eval/analysis.json`
`mde_win_rate_80pct_power`).

## 8. Limitations

- **5x5 DQN was trained for 500,040 steps, short of the planned 2M**, by user decision for
  compute (one seed; `final_dqn/5x5/training_curves_5x5_s42.json`).
- **9x9 DQN was skipped**: 5M steps would take at least 23.2 h
  (`final_dqn/training_timing_5x5_9x9.json`).
- **Observation wall-owner alias:** the DQN input does not show who owns walls that are no longer
  anyone's latest wall, yet HOP needs an opponent's wall; 14.6% of owner-swapped 5x5 audit
  positions change the legal set (`final_dqn/encoding_alias_audit.json`). The trained 3x3 (M = 1)
  and 4x4 (M = 0) cells are not affected.
- **Deterministic agents and distinct openings:** repeated openings replay games, so the
  effective sample is the number of distinct games: 16 per seed on 3x3, 100 for the N5 main runs,
  16 on N9. Older all-games rates (e.g. `final_dqn/tables.md`) are superseded by
  `final_dqn/distinct_reanalysis.json`.
- **Timings under load:** N9 timed Minimax decisions took 359 ms in one run and 522 ms in a rerun
  beside DQN training, with the same 20 games (`final_minimax/vs_greedy_N9_timed_summary.json`,
  `final_minimax/vs_greedy_N9_timed_r2_summary.json`, `final_minimax/n9_r2_load.json`).
- **The pilot's smaller effective n:** its 10 opening seeds held only 8 distinct openings
  (`final_eval/analysis.json` `opening_counts_N5`), so its intervals counted replicas.
- **Capped cells:** the four C-on cells of N4 K1 M1 were not solved; no exact claim is made.
- **Scope:** one Minimax depth and evaluation per row, one board per ablation; exact claims only
  for 3x3 and 4x4.
