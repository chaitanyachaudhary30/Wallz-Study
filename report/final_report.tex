\section{1. Introduction and game rules}

Modified Wallz is a two-player, Quoridor-like pawn race on an N x N grid. Player 0 starts at
\texttt{(0, N//2)} and must reach row N-1; player 1 starts at \texttt{(N-1, N//2)} and must reach row 0.
On each turn a player makes one action. \texttt{wallz\_engine.py} is the only source of the rules
(\texttt{docs/game\_rules.md}, frozen in \texttt{docs/reports/stage1\_engine\_freeze.md}).

\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{longtable}{p{0.45\linewidth}p{0.45\linewidth}}
\toprule
Action & Rule \\
\midrule
MOVE & one orthogonal step; not across a wall, onto the opponent, onto a blocked cell, or off the board; no pawn jump \\
WALL & a 2-cell wall; costs 1 of the \texttt{M} walls; becomes the player's latest wall \\
REPLACE (A) & moves only the player's latest wall; costs nothing; allowed with 0 walls left \\
HOP (B) & once per game; one step across a wall \textbf{owned by the opponent} \\
BLOCK (C) & once per game; a cell is impassable for exactly K plies \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}

Every action must leave a goal path for both pawns (pawns ignored; all blocks count, including
one that expires this ply: team Decision 9). A position whose mover has no legal action is a
draw; a 5-fold repetition is a draw.

\textbf{HOP change.} Until commit \texttt{4e346a8} (2026-11-08) HOP was a 2-cell jump that needed no wall.
Agents used it as a free sprint, so the team switched to the proposal's rule above. Results made
under the old rule are stale (\texttt{STALE.md}).

\textbf{Truncation is not a draw.} The match runner stops a game at 200 plies and records
\texttt{truncated}, its own outcome. Every table here shows draw rate and truncation rate in separate
columns.

\section{3. Agents}

\subsection{3.1 Greedy (Member 2)}

One-ply lookahead on \texttt{H = d\_opp - d\_self} (breadth-first distances), ties broken by action
category, own progress, then action index (\texttt{agents/greedy.py}). Deterministic unless seeded.

\subsection{3.2 Minimax (Member 1)}

Negamax with fail-hard alpha-beta, a distance evaluation \texttt{U = tanh(0.2 (d\_opp - d\_self))},
move ordering and optional iterative deepening under a time limit (\texttt{agents/minimax.py}).
Pruning keeps root values exact: maximum difference 0.0 over 118 positions, and it cuts nodes
from 65,697 to 3,576 on 20 N5 positions at depth 2 (\texttt{final\_minimax/alphabeta\_equivalence.json}).
Move ordering saves 8.4\% of nodes at depth 2 (95\% bootstrap 3.5-14.2\%) on 50 N5 positions
(\texttt{final\_minimax/ordering.json}).

\textbf{Hop-aware scorer — new contribution, Wallz-Study [new].} \texttt{HopAwareEvaluator}
(\texttt{minimax:...,eval=hop}) measures a player's distance as the shortest path crossing at most one
opponent-owned wall while that player's hop is unused. It equals an engine-only search on 240
random positions (\texttt{tests/test\_minimax\_hop\_eval.py}), and the default scorer is unchanged: a full
rerun reproduced all 100 main games (\texttt{final\_minimax\_hop/default\_repro\_diff.json}).

\subsection{3.3 DQN (Member 4)}

Negamax Double DQN with a convolution stack (14 input planes, 64 channels, 256 hidden units),
masked arg-max, sparse +1 reward to the winner, gamma 0.99, self-play against the online
network, snapshots and Greedy (\texttt{docs/report\_sections/member4\_dqn.md}). The action mask equals
the engine's validator on all 5,952,580 ids checked (\texttt{final\_dqn/compat\_mask\_audit.json}).
\textbf{[new]} Retrained under the final rules: 3x3 (K1 M1) seeds 42-46 at 200,000 steps, 4x4 (K1 M0)
seed 42 at 500,000 steps, 5x5 (K3 M10) seed 42 at 500,040 steps (\texttt{checkpoints/final/*/manifest.json},
\texttt{final\_dqn/5x5/final\_dqn\_5x5\_s42\_500k\_eval.json}).

\section{4. Exact solver and evaluation method}

\textbf{Solver.} \texttt{evaluation/state\_graph.py} builds the reachable position graph only through the
engine; \texttt{evaluation/retrograde.py} labels each node WIN, LOSS or DRAW for the side to move with
a rank (plies to the goal under best play) by backward attractor propagation, which handles
cycles. A capped graph reports UNKNOWN, never DRAW (\texttt{docs/report\_sections/member3\_eval.md}, 1-2).

\textbf{Validation [new].} \texttt{tests/test\_solver\_final\_rules.py}, 18 tests, all pass
(\texttt{final\_eval/test\_solver\_final\_rules.txt}); each edge is exactly one engine \texttt{apply\_action}; an
independent DFS + value-iteration reference agrees on every position; two broken engines make
4 and 3 tests fail (\texttt{final\_eval/mutation\_check\_solver\_tests.txt}); the legacy solver agrees on
all 16 N3 cells with 0 mismatches (\texttt{final\_eval/crosscheck\_N3\_K1\_M0.json}, \texttt{crosscheck\_N3\_K1\_M1.json}).

\textbf{Match method [new].} Greedy, Minimax without a time limit and DQN are deterministic, so a game
is fixed by its opening. Openings are random pawn moves; the opening length is the shortest with
enough distinct openings, and only the first seed of each distinct opening is played, in both
seatings. Rates and Wilson 95\% intervals count distinct games. Factorial effects use a paired
bootstrap over openings with Holm correction over the 7 effects.

\section{8. Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{5x5 DQN was trained for 500,040 steps, short of the planned 2M}, by user decision for
compute (one seed; \texttt{final\_dqn/5x5/training\_curves\_5x5\_s42.json}).
\item \textbf{9x9 DQN was skipped}: 5M steps would take at least 23.2 h
(\texttt{final\_dqn/training\_timing\_5x5\_9x9.json}).
\item \textbf{Observation wall-owner alias:} the DQN input does not show who owns walls that are no longer
anyone's latest wall, yet HOP needs an opponent's wall; 14.6\% of owner-swapped 5x5 audit
positions change the legal set (\texttt{final\_dqn/encoding\_alias\_audit.json}). The trained 3x3 (M = 1)
and 4x4 (M = 0) cells are not affected.
\item \textbf{Deterministic agents and distinct openings:} repeated openings replay games, so the
effective sample is the number of distinct games: 16 per seed on 3x3, 100 for the N5 main runs,
16 on N9. Older all-games rates (e.g. \texttt{final\_dqn/tables.md}) are superseded by
\texttt{final\_dqn/distinct\_reanalysis.json}.
\item \textbf{Timings under load:} N9 timed Minimax decisions took 359 ms in one run and 522 ms in a rerun
beside DQN training, with the same 20 games (\texttt{final\_minimax/vs\_greedy\_N9\_timed\_summary.json},
\texttt{final\_minimax/vs\_greedy\_N9\_timed\_r2\_summary.json}, \texttt{final\_minimax/n9\_r2\_load.json}).
\item \textbf{The pilot's smaller effective n:} its 10 opening seeds held only 8 distinct openings
(\texttt{final\_eval/analysis.json} \texttt{opening\_counts\_N5}), so its intervals counted replicas.
\item \textbf{Capped cells:} the four C-on cells of N4 K1 M1 were not solved; no exact claim is made.
\item \textbf{Scope:} one Minimax depth and evaluation per row, one board per ablation; exact claims only
for 3x3 and 4x4.
\end{itemize}
